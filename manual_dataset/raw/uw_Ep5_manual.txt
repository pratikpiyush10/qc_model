① Theme & Narrative Core
The episode is the night the walls fail from the inside. Kaalini doesn't break Suryagarh's protection, she walks through it: the ward-lights die in purple smoke as she passes, the prayer lamps gutter out, and the throne-room doors blast open for her. Agnivesh, disowned and named ill-omened, is the only one who stands in her way, and in each scene he's held back a step too late: by her mockery in the courtyard, by the death of the Priestess Leader in the sanctum, by the spear-wall in the corridor. The core is a stripped prince protecting the people who rejected him. Kaalini's taunts land because they're true (his father erased his name), and he answers with action, not speeches.

**Natural Acting Direction**

Agnivesh: still and contained. His power shows in what he holds back: he doesn't flinch when Kaalini strokes his cheek (28), and his line over the Priestess Leader is quiet (51). He lets go only on the last threat about his father (69).
Kaalini: never shouts. Her cruelty is in her ease: she walks, smiles and almost whispers. Her only raised moments are the triumphant lines in the corridor (67, 70), and even those stay under a scream.
Devika: real fear when the spears swing to her (25), then she stands firm beside him. In the sanctum she's quick and quiet, an organiser rather than a fighter (44).
Temple Priestess Leader: shaking but loud in prayer (37); her last line breaks but doesn't collapse (45).
Royal Guard 3: a messenger out of breath, not a soldier giving a speech (3).
Badrinath: a silent figure by a pillar who doesn't react (56). No look to camera, no guilty acting.
Dialogue: all 26 script lines (30 Devanagari segments) verbatim, at or under 2.8 words per second.
② Visual Style & Framing Tone (Native 9:16 Vertical)
Live-action photorealistic historical Indian epic at night, period practical light only: torches, lamps, ward-lights, the oculus moonbeam and the gold flare of the mark. The grade moves from cold sea-blue (harbour) through gold dying to purple-smoked blue-black (courtyard) and amber and copper going dark (sanctum) to torch pools against moonlight, with violet creeping in (corridor). The cracked dome stays a faint shimmer in every exterior sky.

**Smoke and light rules (Nin's style, applied to every prompt)**

Shadow Hounds trail purple smoke, leak it when hurt, and dissolve into it when killed (the impaled hound dissolves at 5).
Demon smoke is purple, or purple-and-black: Kaalini's entrances (8, 35), the dying ward-lights (10, 15), the smoke across the stones (18), the smoke under the throne-room doors and out of the broken doorway (68, 71–74).
Non-magical props give black smoke only: the prayer lamps gutter out in thin black smoke (36).
The blood seal is black with a blood-red glow pulsing inside it (63).
The courtyard derivative is "Ward-Lights Dark (Purple Smoke)".
**Cinematography Direction**

Native 9:16: at most two people side by side; groups (elites, priestesses, guards) are always staged in depth, never in a line across the frame. Every shot line in the prompts carries a "Sides:" note for the top, middle and bottom thirds.
Faces in the centre third. Spears and dagger lines run vertically or diagonally into depth.
No slow sideways pans. Drama moves are slow push-ins, drifts, arcs, cranes and track-backs.
FAST beats, Marvel-style (15: 11, 25, 26, 32, 40, 42, 44, 46, 47, 51, 53, 58, 61, 67, 71): speed ramps, snap push-ins, fast track-outs and bullet time, exactly as written. The big ones: the leap with the extreme slow-motion sweep past the elites' faces (26), the mark flare (32), the bullet-time flight of the Serpent Dagger with the camera racing backward ahead of its tip (47), the slow-motion spark beat on the spear-wall (61), and the doors blast (71).
Static holds (9: 10, 14, 21, 45, 52, 60, 64, 68, 75) for the ward-light dying, Devika's question, his answer to Kaalini, the Leader's defiance, Kaalini's quiet threat, the spear-wall locking, the chant, the smoke under the doors and the guards' fear.
Screen line: Kaalini and her forces screen-left facing right; Agnivesh and Devika screen-right facing left, the same as Ep 4. The sanctum reverse and the corridor reverse are chosen so this holds.
Lenses: 24–32mm wides with deep focus, 40–50mm mediums, 75–85mm close-ups, 100mm for the inserts (the elite's grip, the smoke at his boots, the wrist, the dagger, the seal, the mask).

**Pacing Direction:** micro-drama pace. Dialogue shots get the line at a brisk read plus a beat; silent and reaction shots 2–3s; establishing wides 3–4s; FAST beats 2–3s (4s only for the leap at 26). ④ Scene-by-Scene Emotion & Camera Intent
**Scene 1 — Harbor Barracks Gate (shots 1–7 / 31s)**

Emotional Target: Spent relief that turns to alarm.
Atmosphere: Sea mist, ash still settling, torches, the cracked dome faint above.
Camera Intent: Start low beside the planted spear so we pick up Ep 4's last image (1), the gateway (2) and Guard 3's report (3). The hound slides off the shaft (4) and dissolves into purple smoke as the camera cranes up with it (5); a slow arc around the pair for "mere saath chalo" (6); a high wide of them running up the shore road (7).
Spatial Staging: Agnivesh outside the gate (Reverse From the Gate Toward the Shore), Devika and the sentries in the gateway, Guard 3 arriving along the shore road in depth.
**Scene 2 — Rajmahal Courtyard (shots 8–33 / 105s)**

Emotional Target: Dread, then defiance under humiliation.
Atmosphere: Gold ward-light choking to purple smoke; cold moonlight; smoke low on the stones.
Camera Intent: Kaalini's intro close-up as she steps out of the smoke (8), a high wide of the elites marching in (9), the first ward-light dying (10). Agnivesh's run in (11, FAST) and the elite's grip insert (12). The standoff plays in singles and two-shots with the elites always in depth behind Kaalini; her taunts are held on arcs and push-ins; the extreme wide that cranes down to a medium for "Raasta chhodo" (20). The spears swing to Devika (25) and his leap (26). Kaalini leaves for the temple (29, East View to the Surya Mandir Steps); the mark flares (32) and they slip away (33).
Spatial Staging: Kaalini screen-left with the elites stacked behind her; Agnivesh and Devika screen-right. The temple steps lie east. The courtyard is "Suryagarh Rajdurg Gate" until the last lights die (8–14), then "Ward-Lights Dark (Purple Smoke)" (15–33, except 29).
**Scene 3 — Surya Mandir Garbhagriha (Inner Sanctum) (shots 34–55 / 87s)**

Emotional Target: Sacred calm broken, then grief.
Atmosphere: Amber lamps guttering out in thin black smoke, incense, the oculus moonbeam, the copper glow of the mandala.
Camera Intent: From the entrance (Inner Sanctum) for the ritual; Kaalini enters through purple smoke on the reverse (35, Reverse From the Altar Toward the Entrance); the reverse for Agnivesh's entrance and the trap (40–43); the rear doorway (Rear Doorway Behind the Altar) for the escape and the death (44–45, 48–51). The killing is restrained: a thin dark line, no spray, and the camera follows her down and stays on the rolling lamp.
Spatial Staging: Priestesses in a ring in depth around the altar; Kaalini mid-sanctum; Agnivesh in the side arch, screen-right; the elites in the main entrance behind him.
**Scene 4 — Grand Corridor (shots 56–76 / 81s)**

Emotional Target: Frustration and urgency, then horror.
Atmosphere: Torch pools and moonlight; the black seal with its blood-red glow; purple-and-black smoke from the throne room.
Camera Intent: The reverse toward the arch (Reverse Toward the Courtyard Arch) for every entrance (Kaalini past Badrinath 56, Agnivesh 58, Devika 62, 65), and the base (Grand Corridor) toward the doors for the striding reveal (57), the spear-wall (60–61), the seal (63–64) and the ritual. The blast (71) is the only shake; then the mask surfacing (72), the warriors stepping out (73) and the armour change (74, Shadow-Fused Armour) on the broken doorway (Throne-Room Doors Blasted Open). Badrinath is visible only in shot 56, as background: no reaction shot and no confrontation.
Spatial Staging: Kaalini mid-corridor; the spear-wall between her and Agnivesh; Royal Guard 2 and the palace guards by the doors in depth; Devika at the arch, then behind a pillar (75).
**Lighting Direction:** only practical, motivated sources. Harbour: moon and torches. Courtyard: gold ward-lights that die in thin purple smoke (they never relight), then moonlight. Sanctum: brass lamp towers and the oculus; the lamps gutter out in thin black smoke. Corridor: torch pools and high windows; the seal's blood-red glow (63–64); from 68, violet light and purple-and-black smoke from under the doors, then from the broken doorway. The bright exception is the mark's gold flare (32–33).

⑤ Sound Direction
Harbour: waves, torches, ragged breath; a long rising hiss as the hound dissolves. Courtyard: marching iron, a hiss as each ward-light dies, then near-silence for the taunts. Sanctum: a chanting hum that chokes off when the lamps die; a slowed, stretched hiss of steel and a heartbeat in the bullet time; the rolling lamp is the loudest sound in the scene. Corridor: echoing footsteps, iron on iron, the seal's wet spreading hiss under Kaalini's chant, the boom of the doors, blades sliding out of forearms.
Dialogue is Hindi in Devanagari, lip-synced, with only the named speaker talking. No music cue is written into the prompts; the score is the editor's.
