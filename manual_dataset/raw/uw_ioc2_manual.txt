IOC2 - DIRECTOR'S MANUAL (Ek Dulhan, Do Rajkumar) - 20 sec

1. THEME / PREMISE

A one-night marriage pact turns into a love triangle. Agnivesh offers Devika a vivaah taadpatra valid for only one night; Devika tests him with a soft confession; Ranvijay bursts in with his sword to stop it. Hook question for the audience: "Aakhir kiski hogi Devika?"

2. VISUAL STYLE & FRAMING

16:9 frame, all key action and faces kept inside the centre 9:16 safe area. Royal Indian period look, warm diya and mashaal light inside the Rajmahal Royal Chamber, deep reds and gold. Devika in red bridal vastra and jewellery; Agnivesh and Prince Ranvijay exactly as their approved character assets. Camera: smooth mechanical moves only (slow dolly, push-in, slow arc, tilt). No handheld, no whip-pans, no crash zooms. Every shot has gentle motion.

3. NARRATIVE STRUCTURE / PACING

0-5s: Setup. Shahi kaksh. Devika stands in bridal red. Agnivesh places the Marriage Scroll (taadpatra) in front of her. AGNIVESH: "Yeh vivaah keval ek raat ke liye hoga!"

5-11s: Intimacy. Devika steps close, takes his hand and places it on her waist. DEVIKA (soft voice): "Aur agar ek raat mein mujhe tumse prem ho gaya toh?"

11-16s: Rupture. Agnivesh looks into her eyes. The chamber door bangs open. Ranvijay stands in the doorway holding the Royal sword. RANVIJAY: "Devika! Tum mere bhai ki patni nahi ban sakti!"

16-20s: Hook. Hold on the three of them. NARRATOR: "Ek dulhan... do rajkumar! Aakhir kiski hogi Devika?"

4. SCENE-BY-SCENE EMOTION

0-5s: Agnivesh calm, guarded, formal. Devika composed, curious.

5-11s: Devika tender, daring; Agnivesh still, caught off guard.

11-16s: Shock. Ranvijay furious and possessive; Devika startled; Agnivesh turns to face him, protective.

16-20s: Tension frozen; triangle composition, Devika in the centre.

5. DIEGETIC SOUND

No music. SFX only: diya flame crackle, soft rustle of bridal fabric and bangles, scroll unrolling on wood, footsteps, heavy door slamming open, sword ring. Narrator voice-over only in 16-20s.

6. TRANSITIONS & CONTINUITY ANCHORS

One continuous location: Rajmahal Royal Chamber. The chamber door is the single entry point; Ranvijay enters only through it.

Positions lock: Agnivesh frame-left, Devika frame-right facing him for 0-11s; when the door opens Ranvijay appears in the background doorway, and in 16-20s the triangle reads Agnivesh left, Devika centre, Ranvijay right. Keep these sides the same in every shot.

Marriage Scroll stays where Agnivesh placed it for the rest of the IOC.

7. DERIVATIVE ASSET PRE-PLAN

Characters: Devika (bridal red look), Agnivesh, Prince Ranvijay. Location: Rajmahal Royal Chamber. Props: Marriage Scroll, Royal sword (in Ranvijay's hand). No other new assets needed.

8. NINNI'S DIRECTOR RULES (apply to every track)

- Dialogue is used exactly as written in the script, word for word. No rewording, no added or removed lines.

- Only the speaking character's mouth moves on their line; everyone else keeps their mouth closed.

- Every shot carries a Positions line; character sides and screen direction stay locked from shot to shot and from track to track. No character suddenly changes side, turns around or faces the wrong way.

- Characters look at the person they are talking to.

- The rendered location must match the location asset exactly, used as the background plate. No invented or redesigned layout, no mixing of conflicting location refs.

- Add every relevant existing asset (each character, location and prop in the shot) as a reference.

- Shot size and camera move follow the Storyboard Table. Shots, shot order and framing are not changed once approved.

- No eye glow on any character.

- Never regenerate an approved asset unless Ninni asks. Devika is never regenerated.
